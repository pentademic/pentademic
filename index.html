Who I Am

I am a final-year MSc Engineering student at École Centrale Méditerranée (France) and a Backend & IoT Apprentice at Locacoeur, a MedTech startup. My daily work involves building and maintaining critical SaaS and MQTT telemetry infrastructures for connected cardiac devices.

My passion lies at the intersection of low-level embedded engineering and modern cloud architectures. I thrive on reducing the gap between hardware constraints and scalable, secure software systems.

My ultimate ambition is to apply my hybrid expertise in Control Theory, Edge AI, and Secure Telemetry to Neuromusculoskeletal Biomechanics — building the next generation of active exoskeletons and bionic prostheses.

Highlighted Work

PROJECT 01
F.I.S.H. D.R.O.N.E. & Edge AI Architecture
Autonomous maritime surveillance drone — STM32H7
  • Designed and deployed highly optimised computer vision models using TinyML on a resource-constrained microcontroller.
  • Comparative study between a multi-output MobileNet architecture and a sequential pipeline (YOLO + CRNN) for object detection and OCR.
  • Quantisation and pruning pipeline via X-Cube-AI to fit model within STM32H7 constraints.
STM32H7 TinyML YOLO MobileNet CRNN X-Cube-AI C/C++
PROJECT 02
M2S — Medicine Management System
Fully automated modular medication dispensing for hospital environments
  • Master/Slave embedded architecture with STM32F7 / Nucleo-G4 coordinating multiple subsystems.
  • Position-control servos, dual RFID tag readers, and embedded camera systems to secure the pharmaceutical circuit.
  • Designed to prevent medication fraud and dispensing errors in hospital settings.
